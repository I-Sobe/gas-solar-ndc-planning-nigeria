## Part A — the rule

> **Every sourced numeric parameter in this model must carry a locator.**
> A citation naming only author, year and journal is insufficient and is
> treated as UNSOURCED. A complete provenance record has six fields:
>
> | field | requirement |
> |---|---|
> | value | the number as used in the model, and where it is set (file:line) |
> | source | author, year, title, journal/publisher |
> | **locator** | table, figure, equation or page number |
> | **quantity** | what the source actually measures — the dependent variable, the units, the population or panel |
> | **range** | the source's own stated range or confidence interval |
> | **fit** | whether the model's value lies inside that range; if not, why |
>
> **The `quantity` field exists because a number can be correctly
> transcribed and still be wrong for its use** — a total-energy
> elasticity applied to electricity, a delivered LNG price compared with
> a wellhead shadow price, a US capex basis applied to Nigeria. Each of
> those has occurred in this project.
>
> **The `fit` field exists because a model value that sits outside its
> source's range is an assumption, not a sourced figure, and must be
> declared as one.**
>
> A parameter whose record cannot be completed is classified ASSUMED
> with a stated direction of bias, never SOURCED.

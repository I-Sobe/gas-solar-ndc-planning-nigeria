### (c) `gas_availability_factor` — application, and the tier question

**Resolution** (identical both builders): annual `:595-604`, sliced
`:514-522`.
```
gas_availability_factor = scenario.get("gas_availability_factor", None)
if gas_availability_factor is None: raise ...
if not (0.0 < gas_availability_factor <= 1.0): raise ...
```
Value comes from `gas_availability_case` (`gas_avail_observed` ~0.80 /
`gas_avail_central` / `gas_avail_nameplate`). It is a plain Python float,
closed over by the constraint lambdas below — **not** a `Param`, **not**
indexed.

**Every application:**

| use | annual | sliced |
|-----|--------|--------|
| gas capacity (energy) constraint — the fleet energy ceiling | `:609` `m.gas_capacity_mw[t] * 8760 * gas_availability_factor / 1e6` | `:708` `m.gas_capacity_mw[t] * H[(s,p)] * gas_availability_factor / 1e6` |
| exempt fuel allowance (only if `new_gas_deliverability=="exempt"`) | `:642` `m.gas_new_capacity_mw[t] * 8760 * gas_availability_factor / gas_new_efficiency / 1e6` | `:742` same with `8760` |

It is **NOT** used in the firm-capacity constraint (that uses
`capacity_credit["gas"]`), nor in the fuel budget, nor emissions.

**Does the structure permit a tier-specific availability alongside the
fleet scalar? — NO, not as-is. It requires a parallel dispatch stream.**

Reasoning:

1. There is exactly ONE gas dispatch variable (`m.gas_to_power`, indexed
   `T` annual / `T×S` sliced) and ONE derived generation expression. The
   fleet energy ceiling `:609/:708` bounds that single stream by the
   single scalar. A tier with PAF 0.106 or 0.960 cannot be represented
   by adjusting one shared coefficient.

2. A blended availability (fleet 0.80 weighted with tier PAFs into one
   number) is the workaround TASK.md forbids inventing — and it would be
   wrong in whichever direction the assumed converted share leans
   (exactly the reason Decision 2 rejects a single aggregate variable).

3. The minimal correct structure — **the same pattern the model already
   uses once**, for `gas_exempt_fuel_allowance` (a parallel gas term
   keyed to a *different* efficiency `gas_new_efficiency`):

   - **New Var** `m.convert_to_power[tier, t (, s, p)]` — thermal
     dispatch of converted plant, `NonNegativeReals`.
   - **New Expression** converted electrical generation
     `= eta_retrofit * m.convert_to_power[...]`.
   - **New Constraint** converted energy ceiling, per tier:
     `<converted gen>[tier, t, ...] <= (1 + uplift) * m.convert_mw[tier]
     * <hours> * PAF[tier] / 1e6` — **PAF[tier] here, not the fleet
     scalar.** This is where tier-specific availability lives.
   - **Amend** the fleet energy ceiling `:609/:708` to net converted
     OCGT nameplate out of the fleet bucket:
     `(m.gas_capacity_mw[t] - sum_tier m.convert_mw[tier]) * <hours> *
     gas_availability_factor / 1e6`. (`gas_availability_factor` itself is
     untouched — TASK.md "Do NOT".)
   - **Amend** `m.gas_balance` (`:645-656` annual / `:745-758` sliced) so
     both thermal streams draw the shared fuel budget:
     `m.gas_to_power[...] + sum_tier m.convert_to_power[tier, ...] +
     m.gas_curtail[t] <= gas_avail[t] (+ allowance)`.
   - **Amend** `m.emissions_grid_only_by_year` (`:1528-1532` /
     `:1526-1531`) to add `<converted gen> * 1e6 * (EF_th /
     eta_retrofit)`.

   Net: **+1 Var, +1 Expression, +1 Constraint per builder, plus edits
   to 3 existing constructs (fleet ceiling, fuel budget, grid
   emissions) per builder.** `gas_availability_factor`, its arms, and the
   `pre_f1_overnight` branch are all untouched.

4. **The one sub-decision that is genuinely Zeus's, not mechanical:**
   in the SLICED builder, is `m.convert_to_power` slice-resolved
   (`[tier, t, (s,p)]`, so converted plant dispatches into the
   dry-season evening peak like the rest of gas) or annual-only
   (`[tier, t]`, a flat converted-energy block)? Slice-resolved is the
   consistent choice — converted plant is dispatchable thermal capacity
   and the whole point of slicing is peak adequacy — but it is a
   modelling call, and it changes the variable count
   (`3 tiers × 30 yr × 6 slices = 540` vs `90`).

   Related: TASK.md pre-decides `m.convert_mw[tier]` **time-invariant**
   (scalar per tier) and asks whether the objective forces vintaging.
   **It does not.** The F1 capital charge (see (d)) discounts a tranche
   over `_df_window_comm["gas"][v]` for vintage `v`; a one-off
   conversion decided at the start assigns `v = 0`, giving a
   well-defined exogenous coefficient `_crf_comm["gas"] *
   _df_window_comm["gas"][0]`. Linear, no vintaging needed. (Vintaging
   would only matter if conversion could be deferred to exploit
   horizon-truncation of the annuity — the 2054-salvage-arbitrage
   pattern — but TASK.md makes it a single one-off decision, removing
   that degree of freedom.)

**Verdict on (c):** tier-specific availability is **expressible**, via
the parallel-stream pattern above, at a cost of ~3 new components + 3
amendments per builder. It is a real structural addition (the first
split of gas dispatch), not a scalar swap, but it is bounded, it has a
direct in-code precedent (`gas_exempt_fuel_allowance`), and it does not
touch any of the TASK.md "Do NOT" surfaces. The only open call for Zeus
is slice-resolved vs annual-only converted dispatch in the sliced
builder. **Per TASK.md I am stopping here for that decision rather than
picking it myself.**

### (d) `gas_add` in the F1 capital charge — the basis conversion capex must follow

Only the `f1_tranche` branch matters (TASK.md forbids touching
`pre_f1_overnight`). Annual `:1341-1357`, `:1425-1431`, `:1469-1474`;
sliced `:1357-1373`, `:1438-1444`, and the rebuild block.

1. **Tranche split, commercial-only.** `m.gas_conc_vol[t]` and
   `m.gas_comm_vol[t]` (`Var(T)`), with **every** `m.gas_conc_vol[t]`
   FIXED to 0.0 (annual `:1351-1352`, sliced `:1367-1368`). Split
   identity (annual `:1353-1357`, sliced `:1369-1373`):
   `gas_conc_vol[t] + gas_comm_vol[t] == scenario["gas_capex_per_mw"] *
   m.gas_add[t]`. With conc fixed at 0, this forces
   `gas_comm_vol[t] == gas_capex_per_mw * gas_add[t]` — 100% commercial.

2. **Capital charge** (annual `:1425-1431`, sliced `:1438-1444`):
   ```
   m.cost_gas_capital_charge_npv = quicksum(
       _crf_conc["gas"] * _df_window_conc["gas"][v] * m.gas_conc_vol[v]     # = 0
       + _crf_comm["gas"] * _df_window_comm["gas"][v] * m.gas_comm_vol[v]
       for v in T)
   ```
   where `_crf_comm["gas"] = _crf(r_comm_real, _L_eff_comm["gas"])`,
   `_L_eff_comm["gas"] = min(_L_comm, round(asset_lifetimes["gas"]))`
   (annual `:1266-1269`, `:1277`; sliced equivalents), and
   `_df_window_comm["gas"][v] = sum(DF[t] for t in v .. min(v+L_eff-1,
   T_last))` — all **exogenous floats** computed before the solve.

3. **Envelope exclusion.** Gas is excluded from
   `m.f1_concessional_envelope_constraint` (annual `:1382-1388`, sliced
   `:1398-1404` — sum is solar_public + solar_eaas + storage conc vols
   only) and from `m.concessional_volume_by_vintage` (annual `:1396-1400`).
   Envelope resolution Decision 1: gas is not concessional-eligible.

4. **Rebuild charge.** `m.cost_rebuild_capital_charge_npv` includes a gas
   term (annual `:1469-1474`) at `_crf_comm["gas"]` for in-horizon
   technical-life replacement of `gas_add` vintages. Conversion, being
   one-off and (per TASK.md) not vintaged, and given retrofit plant life
   likely >= horizon, would not rebuild — but this is where a rebuild
   term would go if Zeus wants one.

5. **Objective assembly.** `m.capital_charge_total_npv` (annual
   `:1478-1486`, sliced `:1491-1499`) sums the four per-channel charges +
   rebuild; `system_cost_npv` uses it under `f1_tranche` (sliced
   `:1587-1594`).

**Implication for step 3.** Conversion capex on the SAME basis as
`gas_add` means: a `m.convert_comm_vol[tier]` (or fold into a
tier-indexed extension of `gas_comm_vol`), an identity
`convert_comm_vol[tier] == capex_per_kw * 1000 * m.convert_mw[tier]`, and
a charge term `_crf_comm["gas"] * _df_window_comm["gas"][0] *

`m.cost_gas_capital_charge_npv` (or a sibling

concessional envelope, NOT in `concessional_volume_by_vintage`. No
concessional draw. `_L_eff_comm["gas"]` and `_crf_comm["gas"]` reused
as-is (retrofit plant is gas plant).

## Step 3 — conversion variables, dispatch stream, capital charge

All in `build_model_sliced()` (`src/optimize_model_sliced.py`), gated by
`_conv = bool(scenario.get("conversion_available", False))`. When
`_conv` is False the builder is byte-identical to pre-2.8: no conversion
component is created and every amended construct falls to an `else`
branch that is a verbatim copy of the original. `build_model()` (annual)
**raises** on `conversion_available=True` -- conversion is slice-resolved
(Decision 3) and has no annual form.

**New components (only when `_conv`):**

| component | shape | meaning |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| `m.convert_mw[tier]` | `Var(A/B/C)`, `bounds=(0, tier nameplate_mw)` | MW of OCGT nameplate converted; time-invariant one-off (Decision 5) |
| `m.convert_to_power[tier, t, (s,p)]` | `Var(A/B/C, T, S)` = 540 | slice-resolved thermal dispatch of converted plant, TWh_th (Decision 3) |
| `m.convert_generation[tier, t, (s,p)]` | `Expression` | `eta_retrofit * convert_to_power` -- the second coexisting gas efficiency (Decision 3) |
| `m.convert_capacity_constraint[tier, t, (s,p)]` | `Constraint` | `convert_generation <= (1 + uplift) * convert_mw[tier] * H[(s,p)] * PAF[tier] / 1e6` -- **tier PAF, not the fleet scalar** (Decision 4); available converted capacity is `(1+uplift)*convert_mw` (Decision 1) |
| `m.convert_comm_vol[tier]` | `Var(A/B/C)` | commercial capital volume, `== capex_per_kw * 1000 * convert_mw[tier]` (`m.convert_split_identity`) |
| `m.cost_conversion_capital_charge_npv` | `Expression` | `sum_tier _crf_comm["gas"] * _df_window_comm["gas"][0] * convert_comm_vol[tier]` -- same basis as `gas_add`, commercial-only, vintage 0 (Decisions 3, 5); NOT in the concessional envelope or `concessional_volume_by_vintage`. Added into `m.capital_charge_total_npv`. |

**Amended constructs.** Decision 4 named three; **the mechanism needs
five** -- flagged here rather than conceded silently:

1. `m.gas_capacity_constraint` (fleet energy ceiling) -- RHS becomes
   `(m.gas_capacity_mw[t] - sum_tier convert_mw[tier]) * H * gas_availability_factor / 1e6`:
   converted OCGT nameplate leaves the fleet-scalar bucket (Decision 4).
   Netted out of the constraint RHS, NOT out of `m.gas_capacity_mw`
   itself (which still feeds land / firm capacity unchanged).
2. `m.gas_balance` (fuel budget) -- both thermal streams draw the shared
   `gas_avail[t]` envelope (Decision 4). All four exempt x conv
   combinations written explicitly.
3. `m.emissions_grid_only_by_year` -- gains
   `converted_generation * 1e6 * (EF_th / eta_retrofit)` (Decision 4).
4. **`m.balance` (per-slice ELECTRICITY balance)** -- converted
   generation is added to the slice it is produced in. **Not in
   Decision 4's list, but mandatory**: without it converted output meets
   no demand, so the LP burns fuel and emits CO2 for nothing and never
   converts -- the feature would be inert. This is the natural
   consequence of Decision 3 (slice-resolved dispatch must enter the
   slice-resolved balance).
5. **`gas_opex_npv`** -- converted thermal draw priced at
   `GAS_COST_PER_TWH_TH`, same as `m.gas_to_power`. **Not in Decision 4's
   list, but mandatory**: omitting it gives converted plant free fuel and
   the LP idles unconverted gas. Same category as the `gas_balance`
   amendment ("the converted stream draws/pays fuel thermally" -- the
   step-0 inventory's (a) note already said `gas_opex` "gains the extra
   dispatch variable in [its] sum").

**NOT amended (Decision 4 did not list them; genuine open items for
Zeus):**

- **`m.firm_capacity_constraint`.** The `uplift * convert_mw[tier]`
  bottoming-cycle capacity does NOT currently count toward the peak
  adequacy constraint. Decision 1 calls the capacity uplift "the larger
  of conversion's two benefits" and Decision 3's rationale is precisely
  peak adequacy -- so this likely wants a follow-up. It was left out
  because (a) Decision 4's amendment list is explicit and does not

# Firm-capacity amendment

Closes the open item flagged at the end of "Steps 1-4 implementation".
Per Zeus's ruling (TASK.md, Phase 2.8 addendum).

## Change

`build_model_sliced()`, `m.firm_capacity_constraint` (annual, `Constraint(T)`).
Under `_conv`, the constraint body gains:

```
+ sum_tier  firm_credit[tier] * uplift * m.convert_mw[tier]
```

with

```
firm_credit[tier] = capacity_credit["gas"] * tier_PAF / gas_availability_factor
```

Only the `uplift * convert_mw[tier]` bottoming-cycle capacity is credited
here. The OCGT nameplate `convert_mw[tier]` is unchanged -- it still
counts through `m.gas_capacity_mw` at `capacity_credit["gas"]` (as does
land). Byte-identical `else` branch for `conv_none`.

The verbatim code comment from the ruling is at the site: since
`capacity_credit["gas"]` and `gas_availability_factor` are assigned from
the same Phase-2.10 local variable, `firm_credit[tier]` reduces to
`tier_PAF` and the scaling applies the model's own existing "firm credit
== availability" convention at tier resolution -- no new assumption.
Inherits the PENDING source status of `capacity_credit["gas"]`
(register L8).

## NOT changed (per the ruling)

- Land constraint -- HRSG island footprint is minor; the OCGT nameplate
  still counts through `m.gas_capacity_mw`.
- `extract_planning_diagnostics` -- the reporting gap is correctly
  identified and correctly left; registered separately (see "Known
  reporting gap" above).

## Residual, noted

The OCGT portion of a converted plant (`convert_mw[tier]`, not the
uplift) remains firm-credited at the fleet `capacity_credit["gas"]`
through `m.gas_capacity_mw`, even though that plant now runs
combined-cycle at tier PAF. For a Tier-C plant (PAF 0.106) this
over-credits the original OCGT capacity relative to how it now operates.
The ruling scopes the amendment to the uplift only, so this is left as
declared behaviour, not corrected.

## Verification (the one permitted execution)

`build_model_sliced()`, NOT solved, `conv_central` + `f1_tranche` +
`cr_central` + `dscr_regression` + `env_dares` (other CLI arms at valid
values). Standard-repn of `m.firm_capacity_constraint[0].body`:

| tier | tier_PAF | firm_credit = PAF x cc_gas / gaf | body coefficient on `convert_mw[tier]` (= firm_credit x uplift) |
|------|----------|----------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| A | 0.9586 | 0.9586 | 0.479303 |
| B | 0.3449 | 0.3449 | 0.172437 |
| C | 0.1057 | 0.1057 | 0.052845 |

All three coefficients present in the constraint body and match the

but they remain equal, assigned from the same local, so `firm_credit`
still reduces to `tier_PAF` exactly as intended.) The pre-existing
solar / gas / storage / hydro terms are unchanged. No solve, no runner.

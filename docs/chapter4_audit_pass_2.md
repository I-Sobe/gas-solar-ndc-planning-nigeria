## Item 6 — F-16 against the hydro bound: no conflict, and one clarification

F-16 says NIRP's 20.6 GW by 2045 is 116.9% of the Energy Commission's 17,620 MW exploitable potential. The model's `hydro_ndc` arm is **not** NIRP's trajectory: `hydro_trajectory('hydro_ndc')` gives 2,989 MW in 2025 rising to **10,400 MW by 2030**, held flat (energy 44.54 TWh_e), the NDC's sourced 10,400 MW target (`src/scenarios.py:1700-1740`); 10,400 MW is 59% of the 17,620 MW potential. F-16 therefore does not make the hydro bound exceed physical potential. The hydro lever's span (13.651 bn, `hydro_ndc` against `hydro_flat`) is an upper bound for two other reasons: hydro is costless in the objective (L95), and the build is 7,411 MW in five years, a peak rate of 1,482 MW per year, 2.1 Zungeru-equivalents per year with "nothing at that scale currently under construction" (the arm's own warning). The bound is an upper bound in time and cost, not in resource. F-16 matters instead for any comparison with NIRP's own hydro path.

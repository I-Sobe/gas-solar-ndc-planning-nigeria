## Amendment 4 — Tightening of the endogenous/exogenous boundary and re-coding of a validation control

Date: 11th October, 2026. Stage: post-extraction, pre-submission.

What changed. The registered protocol (§7, and the worked coding of controls at §5.4) records Agutu et al. (2022) as an endogenous financing treatment, and the registered synthesis design treats Agutu and Oyewo et al. (2018) as bracketing the gap cell from two sides. On full-text reading, Agutu's country- and mode-differentiated costs of capital are empirically derived inputs supplied to the OnSSET optimisation; they are not solved for, and no financing quantity in that study is a decision variable, a solution outcome, or a constraint interacting with the capacity solution. The registered coding was made on the study's abstract-level description and does not survive the full text.

The resulting change to the coding instrument. The endogenous/exogenous boundary has been tightened to the criterion actually applied across the corpus: financing is endogenous only where financing structure or the cost of capital is determined within the model; a supplied value is exogenous however differentiated, derived or swept. Differentiation and derivation are recorded on the separate derivation axis introduced as SQ7. The tightened definition was applied uniformly to all included studies, not selectively to the control.

Direction and significance of the change. The amendment is restrictive on the endogenous category and therefore acts against the review's prior expectation of a sparse rather than empty cell. Under the registered coding, the primary gap probe would have returned one study and the pre-specified interpretation rule would have required the claim to be narrowed from "empty" to "thin or fragmented." Under the tightened definition the probe returns zero. This amendment is accordingly declared in full, with its effect on the headline finding stated, so that the result is read as the product of a stated criterion rather than of a coding decision taken after the fact.

Related deviation, declared here. The registered gap cell was a three-way intersection: endogenous financing × supply-security representation × national capacity-expansion scope (protocol §8). The probe as executed drops the capacity-expansion condition and tests the two-way intersection only. This broadens the conditioning set from a subset of the corpus to the entire corpus of 75 studies, and therefore tests the hypothesis against more evidence rather than less.
